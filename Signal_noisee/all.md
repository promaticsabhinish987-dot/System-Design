# Chapter 1 — Reality Model

### (10 Minutes)

## Core Question


```text

```

```text

```

No signal exists.

# Chapter 8 — Universal Signal Extraction Algorithm

### (10 Minutes)

This is the entire skill.

For anything you learn:

```text
1. What outcome exists?

2. What causes it?

3. Which causes matter most?

4. What repeats?

5. What can be removed?

6. What principle explains it?

7. What system creates it?
```

Every expert thinker—whether in programming, investing, science, chess, or business—runs some version of this pipeline.

The highest-level mastery test is simple:

Give yourself any new topic (Next.js routing, Dynamic Programming, Distributed Systems, Negotiation, Fitness).

Within 5 minutes, answer:

1. What outcome matters?
2. What are the 3 biggest drivers?
3. What pattern keeps repeating?
4. What is the first principle underneath?
5. What can I safely ignore?

If you can consistently do that, you've internalized the skill of signal extraction rather than just learning about it.
